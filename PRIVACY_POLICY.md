## 2. Information We Collect
In **FiguFC 27**, we follow the principle of data minimization: we collect only the technical and account data strictly essential to operate the game, maintain cloud saves, process valid purchases, and serve rewarded advertisements.

The categories of data collected are mapped to Google Play Data Safety standards:

### A. Personal and Account Information
- **User Identifier (User ID):** An anonymous unique identifier generated via Firebase Authentication to link your card inventory, coins, and progress without requiring sensitive personal information.
- **Name and Email Address (Optional - Google Sign-In):** If you voluntarily link your account through Google Play Games or Google Sign-In, we receive your public player name and your registered email address. This data is exclusively used to authenticate your session, sync game saves across cloud storage, and allow account recovery across devices.
- **Profile Picture / Avatar:** If you choose a custom avatar or use your public Google profile picture, this image is processed only to render your player profile inside the game and in multiplayer match rooms.

### B. Device and Advertising Identifiers
- **Device Identifier / Installation UUID:** An anonymous cryptographic identifier generated upon first app launch to handle offline sessions and local cache.
- **Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID):** Automatically collected by the **Google AdMob SDK** to display ads, prevent fraud, and measure campaign analytics.

### C. Game Activity and Progression
- **Gameplay Statistics:** Progress in Career Mode, unlocked cards across album editions, active Squad Draft rosters, Online Division League points, match records, and leaderboard rankings.
- **Market & Showcase Interactions:** Player marketplace listings, card bids, and social trading history within the game.

### D. Financial Data and In-App Purchases (IAP)
- **Google Play Transaction Records:** When you purchase virtual items (such as card packs or virtual coins), the transaction is processed entirely and securely by **Google Play Billing** (Google LLC).
- **What We Do NOT Collect:** **TapGoal Studios NEVER** collects, stores, processes, or has access to credit or debit card numbers, bank details, or financial payment credentials. We only receive cryptographic purchase tokens (anonymous receipt tokens and Order IDs) generated by Google Play to credit the purchased virtual items into your player inventory.

### E. Diagnostics and Technical Performance
- **Crash Logs & Performance Metrics:** Technical details of the operating system (Android OS version, device model, free memory, and crash stack traces) used strictly to debug issues and enhance application stability.
